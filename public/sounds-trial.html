How to report

  1. Under Your phone’s Greek voice, tap ὁ λόγος: that is the voice the rest of the app uses. Note its name.
  2. In each card, tap the buttons across the columns. Play the row plays every clip of a card one after another.
  3. Under Phone voice, the small grey buttons are tricks: the same voice handed different text. Note any that says the bare sound and not the letter’s name.
  4. Say which column sounds most like the voice you hear elsewhere, and which single-letter and drill sounds you would accept.
