# Demo: Lampas is free to read; a licence unlocks the AI (mw-8m9dyu.4)

The epic mw-8m9dyu made Lampas open for anyone: reading, listening, word sheets, lessons and quizzes need no licence, a
shared verse opens for everybody, and a licence unlocks only the AI (the tutor and everything sent to the mill). Without a
licence the AI's controls are not drawn, Settings lists each AI option off with **Needs a licence**, and a **Licence** page
shows this phone's key and **Check again**. This is the Governor's check on his phone. When it all behaves as written, he
says "Looks good".

Needs the installed Lampas on an Android phone with Chrome, on the updated version (reload when the app says "Update
ready, tap to reload"), and a browser profile with no licence: a private tab in Chrome opens
https://lampas.allmymind.org with a fresh key, so it holds none.

## In a private tab, with no licence

1. Open Lampas in a private tab. The Reader opens at **1 John 1**, with no Unlock screen in front of it.
2. Tap a word. Its sheet opens (the word, its gloss, how to say it). Tap **Done**. Nowhere is there **Ask the tutor**,
   **Help with this word**, a **Talk** bar, **Hold to ask**, **Read it aloud** (with its **Hold to read** bar) or a
   **Reading ladder**. Tap a verse number: the verse sheet has only **Listen**, **Copy link** and **Share**.
3. Tap the gear for **Settings**. Under **Goal** pick **Book** **1 John**, then tap **Progress**: the **Goal** screen opens.
   Tap **Learn next**: the idea sheet (a lesson) opens. Back out to **Settings**.
4. In **Settings**, scroll to **Tips**, **The tutor's responses**, **Asking the tutor** (**Ask by**),
   **Hebrew in the tutor** and **My study way** (**Quiz goal**): each option shows **Off**, greyed, so it cannot be turned
   on, with **Needs a licence** beside it.
5. Tap **Needs a licence**. The **Licence** page opens and says **Not licensed**, with **This phone's key** as a QR code and
   as text, **Copy** and **Check again**. The tap on **‹ Settings** goes back.
6. To get a licence today, issue one to that key from Postern's **Key screen** (scan the QR code), then tap **Check
   again** here: the page says **Licensed**, and back in **Settings** the options are live, with no reload.

## Share a verse to someone with no licence

1. On your licensed phone open a verse, tap **Copy link** (or **Share**) and send the link to the private tab.
2. Open the link in the private tab: the Reader opens at that verse and its **Verse view** shows (**Listen**, **Copy link**
   and **Share** only).

## In your own, licensed Lampas

1. Open Lampas as usual. The **Talk** bar, the round **Ask the tutor** button and **Hold to ask** are all there, as before,
   and a verse sheet has **Read it aloud** with its **Hold to read** bar and the **Reading ladder**.
2. Open **Settings**: **Tips** (**On | Off**), **Ask by** (**Speaking | Typing**) and the rest are switches you can use, and
   none says **Needs a licence**. (The **Licence** page, at the address `#/unlock`, says **Licensed** and still shows the key.)
3. Ask the tutor about Romans 8:28: an answer comes, as before.
