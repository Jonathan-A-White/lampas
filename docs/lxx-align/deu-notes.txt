The real-data checks of Deuteronomy are tests/unit/lxx-align-choose-deu.test.ts; the counts and each leftover's reason are data/lxx/align/deu.expect.json; the method is docs/lxx-align.md, 'A pairing pass'.

Deuteronomy 1 to 5 are done (story mw-u2w5klk.349.11: 1,331 runs, `data/lxx/align/deu-01` to `deu-05`, `deu.expect.json`; the English content words unpaired in these five chapters went from 463 to 0, and 97 are settled as rendering no Greek word, each with its reason in the sidecar); the first chapter left is Deuteronomy 6, then 7 to 14, 16 to 30 and 32 to 34 as the ship ships them (chapters 15 and 31 are not shipped). `npm run lxx:unpaired -- --book deu` says what is left at any time.

No fix rows were written: the aligner's own pairs that sit on a neighbouring English word (1:15's 'officers' for χιλιάρχους, 1:31's 'how' and 'any' for the nursing verbs) are named in the sidecar as paired to a neighbour, for a later fix pass.
