# Demo: Lampas starts in English, with a memory per reader (mw-yrmkdf.6)

The epic mw-yrmkdf made Lampas a good Bible in plain English first, with the Greek one tap away. A new licensed reader is
met by the tutor's **Welcome**, a short talk of up to three questions that writes the first lines of his **Memory** and keeps
the Reader in English. Later the tutor offers the Greek once, gently, and only when the reader shows real interest; a "no" is
remembered and the offer does not come again until he asks for the Greek. **Settings > Memory** shows everything Lampas keeps
about him, on his phone, to edit, delete, **Export** and **Import**. This is the Governor's check on his phone. When it all
behaves as written, he says "Looks good".

Needs the installed Lampas on an Android phone with Chrome, on the updated version (reload when the app says "Update ready,
tap to reload"), a licence (the AI is part of what is checked) and a connection to the tutor.

## Start as a new reader

1. Clean start: open **Settings** (the gear), then **About**, and tap the version number seven times: a note says
   **Developer mode is on**. Back in **Settings**, under **Developer mode** make sure it is **On**, then tap **Show the welcome
   again**. This forgets the welcome talk, puts the English defaults back and makes Lampas welcome you the next time it opens.
   (Do this again to run the demo from the top. It does not clear **Memory**: for a bare index, open **Settings > Memory** and
   delete what is there first.)
2. Close Lampas and open it again. The **Reader** shows **1 John 1** in English only: no Greek lines, no chips about words, and
   the **English | Greek** choice at the top of the Reader is on **English**. At once a sheet titled **Welcome** opens, with a
   **Skip** button in its header, and you have said "Hello, I have just started using Lampas."
3. The tutor answers with one plain-English question, no Greek in it: why you are here (to read, to study, or to learn the
   Greek). Answer "just read" (type it in **Your message** and tap **Send**, or hold the mic bar). It asks, one at a time, what
   you want from the app, then one thing about yourself. Answer each in a few words. After at most three questions it thanks
   you and the welcome ends; the sheet stays up to be read. Close it. The Reader is still English only.
4. Open **Settings**, then **Memory**. Under **Index** are the lines the tutor wrote from your answers (that you came to read,
   what you want, the one thing about yourself), with "Changed <date> by the tutor" under the box. Nothing about Greek yet.

## One gentle offer, and a remembered no

1. In the Reader tap a verse number, choose **Ask the tutor** in the verse sheet, then tap **Talk about verse N**.
2. Ask what a word really means, for example "What does the word for 'love' really mean here?". The answer comes in plain
   English with no Greek words in it and, under it, ONE line as a button, such as "Show me the Greek word" (it is the only
   offer in this talk).
3. Do not tap it. Say no: type "No thanks" and tap **Send**. The button goes away.
4. Ask two more questions about the verse. Neither answer carries an offer of the Greek.
5. Open **Settings > Memory**. The **Index** now holds a line "said no to the Greek offer" and today's date, your phone's own
   day (the date a day ahead in the evening was mw-yrmkdf.9, fixed).

## Greek, when you ask for it

1. Back in the verse's talk, say "Show me the Greek". The tutor answers and shows the Greek (its button, or the Reader's
   **Greek** choice at the top of the Reader, which you can also tap yourself: the Greek is one tap away for everybody). Once
   the Reader is in Greek, tapping a word opens its sheet as it always has.
2. In **Settings > Memory** the line "said no to the Greek offer …" is gone from the **Index**: you asked, so the tutor may
   offer again. (Put the Reader back to **English** at the top of the Reader to leave the demo as it started.)

## Memory in Settings

1. Open **Settings > Memory**. At the top: "What Lampas keeps about you, on this phone only: an index, and notes about a book,
   a word or a subject. You can change or delete any of it." Under **Index** is the box you can type in, saved as you type; under
   it "Lampas adds this list from your notes:" with one line per note, if there are any.
2. Edit a line of the **Index**: change a word and see that the line under the box now says **Changed <date> by you**.
3. Under **Notes** (the list is there only when the tutor has made a note, such as `book:1 John` or `topic:…`, from your talks;
   ask it a few more questions about a book or a word if it is empty) tap **Delete** beside one. Lampas asks **Delete this
   note?**; tap **Yes, delete** and the note goes. (A note is also **Edit**-able in place; Lampas never adds one on your behalf
   from here.)
4. Under **Keep a copy** tap **Export**: the phone saves one file, `lampas-memory-<date>.json`.
5. Edit the **Index** again (add a line "TEST"), then tap **Import** and pick that file. Lampas says what the file holds
   ("The file holds the index and N notes: …") and changes nothing until you tap **Replace my memory**. Tap it: the **Index**
   is back as it was at the export, without "TEST". (A file that is not a Lampas memory is refused and changes nothing.)

## The notes the tutor wished he had

1. Open **Settings > About** and scroll under the credits. When the tutor has named a note that would have helped it
   answer (a `book:`, `word:` or `topic:` name) and Lampas did not have it, a line says **Notes the tutor wished he had: N**
   with the names under it. With no such note nothing is drawn: the count is not shown at **0**.
2. To see one, ask the tutor a question about a book or word it would want a note on (for example "Remember what I told you about
   Romans?" when no `book:Romans` note exists) and come back to **About**. The Governor says "Looks good".
