# Demo: Postern's composer, shared with Lampas (mw-jtzpw0.6)

The epic mw-jtzpw0 lifted Postern's message composer into bsv-kit (`bsv-kit/composer`). Lampas's **Ask the tutor** now uses it in
place of the old text box, **Ask** button and separate **Hold to ask** bar, a setting **Ask by** makes it speak-first or
type-first (and the tutor can switch it when he asks), and in Postern a tapped picture opens full screen. This is the
Governor's check on his phone. When it all behaves as written, he says "Looks good".

Needs a phone with a microphone and a speech recogniser (Chrome on Android). Run each app on the updated version (reload when
the app says "Update ready, tap to reload"). Lampas needs a connection to the tutor; Postern needs a conversation with the
Mayor.

## Lampas: ask by voice

1. Open **Romans 8** and tap a verse number (**Verse 28**). The **Verse view** opens over the reading.
2. In the row of actions tap **Ask the tutor**. At the foot of the screen is Postern's composer: one big gold bar,
   **Hold to ask**, and under it a quiet **Type a question**. There is no **Ask** button, no second bar, and no attach or
   camera (the tutor takes no file).
3. Hold **Hold to ask** and say a question ("What does this verse mean by works together?"). The bar says **Release to
   send** and his words appear above it as he speaks. Slide your finger off the bar before letting go: it says **Let go to
   keep it unsent** and the words wait for you in the box instead.
4. Hold it again, say the question, and let go. The question shows with **Waiting for the tutor…**, then the answer card
   comes, with his question above the answer.

## Lampas: Ask by Typing

1. In the same **Ask the tutor** panel, tap **Type a question**. The text box comes out ("Type a question") and a **Send**
   button once there are words. Type a question and tap **Send**; the tutor answers as before.
2. Ask the tutor to switch: hold **Hold to ask** and say "Let me type instead". The answer says it has switched **Ask by**
   to Typing and shows **Changed: Ask by: Typing** under it. The panel is now type-first: the text box and **Send** are
   there at once, with a small microphone ("Ask by speaking") beside them, and no big bar.
3. Tap the gear to open **Settings** and find **Ask by** under **Asking the tutor** (or type "Ask by" in **Search
   settings**). **Typing** is chosen. Choose **Speaking**; tap **‹ Reader**, open the Verse view and **Ask the tutor**
   again: the big **Hold to ask** bar is back, with **Type a question** under it.
4. Choose **Typing** in **Settings** again, then close the app and open it: the panel is still type-first. Tap the small
   microphone ("Ask by speaking") and the **Hold to ask** bar comes out for this question.
5. Leave **Ask by** on the one he likes.

## Postern: a tapped attachment

1. Open **Channels**, then **Talk to the Mayor** (or any channel).
2. Tap **Attach files** (or **Take a photo**) and choose a picture. It waits in the row **To send** as a small thumbnail.
3. Tap the thumbnail (its name is **View** and the file's name). The picture opens full screen over everything, dark, with a
   **Close picture** button at the top. Pinch to zoom, and double-tap to zoom in and out. Tap **Close picture**, or Back:
   the composer is as you left it, the picture still waiting.
4. Send the message. In the sent message tap the picture (**View picture**). It opens the same full-screen view, and
   **Close picture** or Back closes it.
5. Attach an ordinary file (a PDF, say) and tap it, before sending (**Open** and the file's name) or in the sent message: the
   file opens or downloads instead of a picture view.

## Right

- In Lampas the panel is Postern's composer: the same big bar and quiet **Type a question**, in Lampas's own gold; his
  words appear as he speaks; nothing else sits beside it.
- **Ask by** Speaking is bar first, Typing is text box first, the setting is kept across a reload, and asking the tutor to
  switch it does the same as the setting and says so (**Changed: Ask by: Typing**).
- In Postern a tapped picture opens full screen both before sending and in a sent message, and **Close picture** (or Back)
  brings him back to where he was.

Then: "Looks good."

## Known differences (his word decides which become stories)

- Lampas's tutor takes no pictures or files, so Lampas's composer has no **Attach files** or **Take a photo**; tapping an
  attachment is a Postern check only. (Lampas's Bible talk sheet has its own picture controls, which are not this composer.)
- The Postern picture viewer's **Save** button is a separate story (mw-jtzpw0.7) and may not be there yet.
