## The languages and their dials (mw-3ased2.2, mw-gdcyf98.2)

The first thing on the home, under the search field, is **Which languages?**: English only, Greek, Hebrew or Greek and Hebrew (`controls/languages.tsx`, four 44 px buttons; label and hint PROVISIONAL). It is the setting `languages` (registry.ts `languagesEntry`, section `dial`, **no store of its own**): reading it answers `languagesOf(greek, hebrew)` from the two dials (english while both are 0); writing it moves them (`setLanguages`, `src/data/repositories/greekInterest.ts`): english puts both at 0, greek puts Hebrew at 0 and raises Greek from 0 to the level kept under `greekKept.level` (the level a dial left for 0; else 1), hebrew the mirror (`hebrewKept.level`), both raises each dial at 0 the same way (the higher dial first, so his kept values win over the steps' starting values). The tutor may read and change it like any setting.

Under it is a **dial per language**, drawn only while its language is chosen (their rows `dependsOn` `languages`, through the kit's one rule): **How much Greek?** (`greekInterest`) and **How much Hebrew?** (`hebrewInterest`), each a choice of `'0'` to `'5'` with the same six steps, 0 Just English, 1 Curious, 2 Dipping a toe, 3 Learning, 4 Reading in Greek (Hebrew), 5 All in (`interestSetting(lang)`, `levelsOf(lang)` in `src/settings/definitions/interest.ts`; `GREEK_LEVELS` is `levelsOf('grc')` re-exported by `greekInterest.ts`, `HEBREW_LEVELS` is `hebrewInterest.ts`'s; names and lines PROVISIONAL, from the epic mw-3ased2; every line names its own language only). `controls/interest.tsx` draws either dial as five 44 px buttons (step 0 is **never** a button: English only of the row above is how a language is left off) with the chosen step's line under them; `controls/greekInterest.tsx` re-exports it. A phone not yet placed reads Greek 5 (nothing hidden) and Hebrew 0; `placeInterest` (`src/main.tsx`, before the first render) places both once.

- **What each step shows** is the ordinary `dependsOn` rule: a Greek row says `dependsOn: greekFrom(n)` (shown from Greek step n up: Greek voice, speed, Pronunciation, Greek keyboard, the Greek study resources, Grammar approach, Handwriting drills until a Hebrew approach and Hebrew strokes exist); a Hebrew row `hebrewFrom(n)` (Hebrew in the tutor, from 1, in the Learning group's `hebrew` section); a row both languages share says `depthFrom(n)`, the key **`depth`**, which `readValues` (`rows.ts`) answers as the higher of the two dials exactly as it answers `developerMode` (no second visibility mechanism: `tests/unit/greek-interest.test.ts` proves every row at every pair of levels 0..5 x 0..5). 1: Words and Review; 2: the Weave rows; 3: New words a day (and its details), Read lessons to me, Quiz goal; the Learning page appears; 4: Goal; 5: Paradigms, How much of a table. A detail of one of those (Order and Grammar under the Weave, New words at and Move it under New words a day) is hidden through the chain. At 0 and 0 no Greek or Hebrew row, no Learning page and no word sheet is drawn. A search for a shared row that the dials hide lists the dial that is drawn in its place (`rows.ts` `visibleRows`), or Which languages? when none is. The study resources follow the Greek dial by language (`lang` on each, `src/resources/resourcesForLevel.ts`: Greek ones hidden at 0, English first between; `docs/resources.md`). `tests/unit/greek-interest.test.ts` lists the table; `tests/unit/languages.test.ts` the two tables, the four reads, the writes and the migration; `features/languages.feature` the screen.
- **What the Reader does with it**: `viewAt`, `weaveAt` and `newWordsAt` (greekInterest.ts) clamp what is saved: the Reader (`useReaderSettings`, `useViewSettings`), the Language switch (`Reader.tsx`), the word sheet (`WordSheet.tsx`) and the pace (`usePace.ts`) read the Greek dial, so what is hidden does not act (they read the Greek dial alone until a story gives the Reader a Hebrew one). A row a link opens that a dial hides says which step shows it (`neededText`, `SettingsScreen.tsx`: for a Hebrew row "choose Hebrew at the top of Settings, then raise “How much Hebrew?”", Greek likewise, for a depth row "raise either dial").
- **Moving a dial** is `setGreekInterest` / `setHebrewInterest` (`src/data/repositories/greekInterest.ts`, one `moveDial`): the settings the steps change follow the **higher** dial. Up, each step the higher dial passes brings in its starting values (Weave Solid at 2, 3 new words at 3, the Greek view, Greek word order and 5 new words at 4); down, what the lower step hides is turned off only when **both** dials stand below its step (the Greek view, the Weave, new words a day, Handwriting drills, Read lessons, Quiz goal, Goal) after his own value is kept under `greekKept.<key>`; going back up restores it (his value wins over a starting value). A dial that goes to 0 keeps its level under `greekKept.level` / `hebrewKept.level` for Which languages? to put back. The Greek voice, speed, pronunciation, Grammar approach, New words at, Move it and Hebrew's depth have no Off of their own and stay as they are. The welcome's English defaults are step 0 of both dials (`putEnglishDefaults`: `setHebrewInterest(0)` then `setGreekInterest(0)`).
- **Where a phone starts**: a phone with nothing of a reader kept starts both dials at 0 and nothing else is written; a reader from before is placed by what he has (Greek view on 4, new words above 0 is 3, Weave on 2, else 0) and his Hebrew dial is **written equal to his Greek one, once** (everything Hebrew hung off the Greek step before, so nothing he could see vanishes: PROVISIONAL). The `lampas.greekInterestStart` and `lampas.hebrewInterestStart` keys in localStorage (docs/testing.md) are a test's way to start a phone at another step; the Hebrew one, absent, follows the Greek one.
- **The generator's cap**: the bible-talk instructions list every setting (`src/settings/grindText.ts` `settingsBlock`) and sit under their 99,500-byte ceiling: a setting whose help is longer than 260 bytes (plain text) gives way to its hint there (`HELP_MAX_BYTES`; `HELP_KEPT` names the ones kept whole: Which devotional, Weave, Ask by); settings that differ only in their language (the voices, the speeds) are one line (mw-gdcyf98.28), so a long help is for the screen and the hint is what the tutor reads; its Value list still names every step.
- The dials' section `dial` is in `HOME_SECTIONS` (`rows.ts`; titled **Your languages**), in no group: `groupOf` throws for it, so a caller of `groupOf` over every row filters `isHomeRow` first.

## The home and the groups

`#/settings` is the home: a search field pinned under the header ("Search settings"), then Which languages?, the dial of each language chosen and the groups, each a button that opens its page. **Your Logos library** is the first row of Study resources (mw-5r3p30.837; mw-5r3p30.752 had put it on the home): label `Your Logos library`, a count line `Your Logos library · N books on`, a tap opens `#/library` through `openLibrary()`. It is the row `library` of the section `resources` (listed first in `unordered`, so first in the section, above the switches and Bible in Logos; `HOME_SECTIONS` is the `dial` alone), drawn by `LibraryControl` (`controls/resources.tsx`) whether Logos is On or not (the Library also holds Verse lists and Memorize); the group's hint names it. It is no setting (`isTalkable` false), but `src/settings/link.ts` gives it a link, `#/settings?setting=library` (`DOOR_ROWS`): `openSetting('library')` and the Settings search's result for it ('Your Logos library', under Study resources) open the Study resources page with the row in view and marked.

The table is `GROUPS` in `src/settings/rows.ts`: one entry per group with its `sections`, and `groupOf(section)` reads it. A section is in exactly one group (`tests/unit/settings-groups.test.ts` fails on a section in none or two, and on an empty group). `visibleGroups(values)` is what the home lists: the groups with a row drawn.

A group's page is `#/settings?group=<id>` (`groupLink`, `groupIdOf`, `openSettingsGroup` in `src/settings/link.ts`; ids `reading greek tutor listening daily study account developer`). It has the group's title, the `‹ Settings` back arrow (`backToSettingsHome`: a step Back when the home is the entry behind, else the home in its place) and the group's sections in `SECTIONS` order. The phone's Back from the home leaves Settings, as before. Each page keeps its own scroll place (`scrollAddress` in `src/nav/scrollMemory.ts`).

## Keep on this phone (mw-lcv9z3g.2)

The row `offlineCopy`, **Keep on this phone**, is in the section `reading` (Settings > Reading, `OFFLINE_COPY_ROW` in `rows.ts`, control `controls/offlineCopy.tsx`, the row itself `src/settings/OfflineCopyRow.tsx`). It is a row, not a registry setting: the scope is the offline copy's own key `offlineCopy` in the settings store (`src/data/offlineCopy.ts`, docs/data.md 'The offline copy'), so the tutor neither reads nor changes it (`isTalkable`; its Settings request is full). Three choices (chips, `OFFLINE_CHOICES`): **Nothing extra** (to start), **New Testament (34 MB)**, **Whole Bible (113 MB)**. Choosing one saves it and starts the run (`chooseOfflineScope`, `offlineCopyState.ts`, which also holds `OFFLINE_CHOICES` and `statusOf`); Nothing extra stops the run and deletes nothing; a phone that chose a scope before checks it again when the row first shows unless a run is going (`resumeOfflineCopy`, which is `refreshOfflineCopy`, the check the app also makes at its start: what is kept is not fetched again). Under the chips the row says, by the run's progress (`statusOf`, `[data-offline-status]`):

1. **Running**: `N of 260 chapters · 12 MB` (chapters kept, megabytes fetched by this run) and the line `Keep the app open`.
2. **Paused**: `Paused: offline` (above the count, while the phone is offline; the run goes on at 'online').
3. **Up to date**: `Up to date · checked <time>` once every file is kept (the clock for today, else the day and the clock; the time is the offline copy's own, `getOfflineCheckedAt`, written as a check starts). If a file failed twice the row says `Could not keep N of M.` and `Choose it again to try them again.`

Nothing is said under Nothing extra. `features/offline-copy.feature`, `tests/e2e/offline-copy.spec.ts` (the chapter files are 20 KB stand-ins so the run is fast; the 260 addresses are the real ones).

## Search

The kit does the work (mw-79en7t.7): `defineSetting`, the registry (`createRegistry`, `entryOf`), `rowsInOrder`, `visibleRows`, `neededFor`, `rowsAt`, `visibleGroups`, `searchSettings` and the search field, group button and found rows are `pwa-kit/settings`'; `src/settings/define.ts`, `registry.ts`, `rows.ts` and `SettingsHome.tsx` keep Lampas's own (its section ids, `SECTIONS`, `GROUPS`, the settings, the links, where a tap goes, the books found) and give the kit's functions Lampas's list and its Markdown-to-words (`helpPlain`). Its look is `pwa-kit/settings/styles.css`, themed in `src/index.css` ('pwa-kit's Settings home'). A setting's `defineSetting` throws at start-up for a default it does not allow.

Typing on the home swaps the groups for the rows that match by name or hint (`visibleRows`; a detail hidden by a setting that is off is found through the row that turns it on). Each result names its group; choosing one opens its page at the row, scrolled to and marked (`openFoundRow`, `src/settings/link.ts`: a setting of the registry, the library door and a row that is no setting, such as Keep on this phone, a link row or the devotional, alike; `settingKeyOf` reads the address of any row of `ROWS`, while `settingLink`, which the tutor's links use, stays the registry's). A row taller than the screen (Grammar approach) is scrolled to by its top (`bringRowIntoView`). `tests/e2e/settings-search-lands.spec.ts` taps every result. The field keeps its text when he comes back to the home.

## Links into Settings

- `#/settings?setting=<key>` (`settingLink`, `openSetting`): the page holding that row, at the row, marked until his next tap; the row is drawn whatever its dependency would hide, with the setting to turn on first beside it (`rowsAt`, `neededFor`).
- `#/settings?section=<id>` (`openSettingsSection`): the page holding that section, scrolled to it. The Goal screen's Choose a goal and Change (`goal`), the reference card's and the link notice's Link Logos in Settings (`resources`) use it.
- A screen that returns to Settings by `backTo('settings')` (About you, Memory, My study way, Ask for more, Placement) steps Back to the page it came from.

## The Library screens (mw-rfjjzd.4)

`#/library` (route `library`, `src/library/LibraryScreen.tsx`, 'Your Logos library') is a screen of its own, not a Settings page: the card (how many of the catalogue's books are on), a search over every book's name, title, author and series, and seven rows, one for each kind (`KIND_INFO`, `src/library/kinds.ts`), each with its count from the shelf ('3 on · 1 pinned', 'none on', Bibles '1 chosen: LSB'). `#/library?kind=lexicon` (`KindPage.tsx`) is a kind's page: its help line, All on / All off (no All on for Bibles), and the books in a `SearchableList` grown for it (`flat`, `row`, `rankOf`, `status`, `focus`): pinned, on, off, then name, ranked when the page opens and when the search changes, never as he taps. Each row's control is `src/ui/ThreeStep.tsx` (a radiogroup named by the book, each step 44 px, through `oneTap`): Off | On | Pin, Off | On for Devotionals, Off | Chosen for Bibles. A pin the shelf refuses (a kind holds `MAX_PINS`, 3) puts `pinRefusal()` ('Three pinned already: unpin one first') under the book as an alert. A search result opens `#/library?kind=<kind>&book=<Resource ID>` (`openLibrary`, `libraryOf`, `src/nav/route.ts`): the page opens with that book in view and marked (aria-current). The home's back is `backTo('settings')`, a kind page's `backTo('library')`; the screens report their counts to the tutor (`SCREEN_NAMES.library`). Its door in Settings is the first row of Study resources (mw-5r3p30.837); the word sheet's Manage in Your Logos library is the other. The shelf is read live through `src/library/useShelf.ts`; the table is touched only in `src/data/repositories/library.ts`. Proof: `features/library.feature`, `tests/e2e/library.spec.ts` (390x844: 44 px, no sideways scroll, the three hit areas), both screens in `large-text.spec.ts` (MORE_SCREENS) and `accessibility.spec.ts`.

## Adding a setting to a group

1. Declare the setting (`src/settings/definitions/<name>.ts`, one line in its `index.ts`, or an entry in `src/settings/registry.ts`) with its `section`, and write its control in `src/settings/controls/` (`CONTROLS`, `index.ts`).
2. Its page is the page of its section's group: nothing else to do. To put a setting on another page, change its `section`.
3. A new section: add its id to `SectionId` (`define.ts`), an entry in `SECTIONS` (`rows.ts`) and its id to the `sections` of one group in `GROUPS`; the unit test fails until it is in exactly one.
4. A new group: an entry in `GROUPS` (id, title, hint, sections) and its id in `GroupId`.
