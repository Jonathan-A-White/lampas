# Layout: The chapter picker

Which file holds what, for the chapter picker the Reader's title opens: the 66 books of the Bible in canon order, the chapters of the one he taps, a typed reference, and how a chapter of either testament opens in the Reader.

Moved out of docs/layout/reader.md (mw-uqlxea.2). A story that adds or changes a file of this area writes its note here, in the same form (the path, then `#` and what it holds; a continuation line starts `#   `).

```
src/ChapterPicker.tsx # the chapter picker (mw-5r3p30.60), opened by the Reader's title: a bottom sheet (useSheetBack) with the 66 books of the Bible in canonical order, Genesis to Revelation (src/data/books.ts BOOKS, searchable, in a box of its own; rows are [data-book]; mw-uqlxea.2 folded the Old Testament in: an Old Testament book is a row like any other, with no 'Opens in Logos' tag); the search also takes a reference, mw-5r3p30.622: 'John 18:36', 'jn18', '1 Jn 2:1', 'Ps 23' read by readPlace in src/nav/parseReference.ts, the one reader of a typed place (a layer on links.ts' parseTypedReference over parseCanonReference, its one table of names; mw-5r3p30.683: the book may be any unique start of a name, 'ep 4:15', 'Jo 3:16': an exact name first, else the one New Testament book it fits, else the one Old Testament book, else nothing is guessed ('J 3', 'Ph 1'); only readPlace is loose, the readers of free text keep parseCanonReference; tests/unit/parse-reference.test.ts fails on a second table); the list filters by letters in order, names that start with what he typed first (ui/listFilter.ts matchingInOrder: 'phlm' finds Philemon), by the name alone when a chapter follows it (parseReference.ts bookPart), Enter or the 'Go to' row opens the chapter, a verse (or the first of a range) through openAtVerse / bringVerse in src/nav/readerRequest.ts, not the Verse view (mw-5r3p30.678: the Reader opens on the chapter and src/reader/useBringVerse.ts scrolls the reading box so that verse stands at its top, on the bus as `reader-requested` action 'verse', and sets data-brought-verse on the box; no verse is selected), and a chapter past the end says 'John has 21 chapters'; a book's name alone still filters) then a 4-column grid of the book's chapters (the New Testament's counts from loadIndex, data/index.json; the Old Testament's from src/data/books.ts, no fetch); a chapter of EITHER testament opens through openReader({book, chapter}) as a new Back step (the Reader is a ReaderBody keyed by book/chapter, so everything starts afresh); an Old Testament chapter is read in English only (docs/layout/reader.md 'An Old Testament chapter (English only)'), and the two ways out the picker once offered under its grid (mw-5r3p30.496, a panel [data-ot-choice]) are 'Read in Logos' and 'Listen in Audible' on the opened chapter (src/reader/ChapterActions.tsx), not here; in choose mode (mw-umv8sp.3, the return sheet's Pick it myself) a tap on any chapter, of either testament, hands it to onChoose and opens nothing; a chapter that cannot be fetched says 'is not on this phone yet, and you are offline' with Try again and Choose another chapter
src/data/books.ts canon.ts # the 66 books of the Bible (mw-uqlxea.2): canon.ts holds the table (code, name, chapter count, `lang` 'hbo' for the 39 of the Old Testament and 'grc' for the 27 of the New, and the book's abbreviation in a Logos reference, Logos' own list); books.ts is the door: BOOKS (all 66, Genesis to Revelation), NT_BOOKS and OT_BOOKS (its two halves), bookOf (any code), ntBookOf and otBookOf (one testament; a screen that still holds only the New Testament asks ntBookOf), isHebrewBook and titleOf. src/data/otBooks.ts is gone, folded in. tests/unit/books.test.ts holds BOOKS equal to public/data/index.json's 66
src/resources/oldTestamentChoices.ts # what an Old Testament chapter offers outside Lampas (mw-5r3p30.496): the chapter's Logos link (logosBible.ts chapterLink in his Bible in Logos, setting 'logosBible', default LSB; absent with Logos off) and Audible's link at the start of the chapter with Audible's own name for it ('Judges 3'; absent with the Audio Bible off, remade at the tap); pure; tests/unit/old-testament-choices.test.ts holds the same links the picker once made. Drawn by src/reader/ChapterActions.tsx
```
