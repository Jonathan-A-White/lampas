# Layout: The word sheet, Greek and Hebrew

The bottom sheet a tapped word opens (src/WordSheet.tsx), for a word of a Greek chapter and, since mw-5vvlay.2, of an Old Testament chapter (an 'hbo' chapter, whose words are Hebrew). The Greek card's note is in docs/layout/reader.md
(its `src/WordSheet.tsx` line); this file is the Hebrew sheet and what the two share. A story that changes the sheet writes its note here.

