# Layout: The word sheet, Greek and Hebrew

The bottom sheet a tapped word opens (src/WordSheet.tsx), for a word of a Greek chapter and, since mw-5vvlay.2, of an Old Testament chapter (an 'hbo' chapter, whose words are Hebrew). The Greek card's note is in docs/layout/reader.md
(its `src/WordSheet.tsx` line); this file is the Hebrew sheet and what the two share. A story that changes the sheet writes its note here.

```
src/WordSheet.tsx # the one sheet, two cards. WordSheet gates on the Greek dial (Curious up draws it; below, an English tap opens EnglishWordSheet, with `listWords` in an 'hbo' chapter) and WordSheetBody picks the card by sourceLanguage(chapter): WordCard for Greek, HebrewCard for Hebrew (mw-5vvlay.2, features/hebrew-word-sheet.feature, features/hebrew-reader.feature, features/old-testament.feature)
src/WordSheet.tsx HebrewCard # a Hebrew word: Listen (he-IL, label 'Listen', at the left) and the pointed form large and right to left (`sheet-word`, lang he, dir rtl, `.script-he`, text-4xl); its transliteration where the Greek has the respelling (`sheet-transliteration`, the word's `tr`, with the link actions: Copy link / Share, #/?word=H7225); then the facts: Lemma (`sheet-lemma`, rtl), Parsing (`sheet-parse`: the chapter's parse map, 'Verb - Qal - Perfect - third person masculine singular', ONE plain stretch: parseSegments(parse, 'hbo') in src/data/parseCode.ts makes no term a link, since the Grammar sheet explains Greek terms), Meaning (the gloss of the chapter's lex), Definition (`sheet-definition`, the lex's `d`: only a word of the open chapter has one, lexicon-hbo.json has gloss and part of speech only), Strong's ('H7225', a fact of its own only while the Strong's resource is off, as for Greek), English (the chunk of a Hebrew tap); then the Study row (below) and, while Logos is off, the Turn on Logos line (the first card only)
src/WordSheet.tsx # an English tap in an 'hbo' chapter (the chunk's `g` are Hebrew words) opens the same sheet headed with the English (`sheet-heading`), a HebrewCard for each Hebrew word of the chunk, and under them EnglishWordList (src/EnglishWordSheet.tsx: each English word of the chunk with its Listen and its Look it up in Logos, Webster's 1828, mw-uqlxea.2); a Greek chunk still ends with the one Look it up in Logos (English) row. A Hebrew word the English has no chunk for (אֵת) opens its sheet all the same
src/WordSheet.tsx # LATER for a Hebrew word, not drawn: the Paradigm row (no Hebrew paradigms yet), the Heart of a word note (keyed by Greek lemma), Help with this word / Ask the tutor and the grammar-term links of the Parsing (the tutor and the Grammar sheet know Greek only: mw-5vvlay.3 and the learning-Hebrew epic), the respelling (Hebrew has the transliteration and Listen instead), the Lexham Theological Wordbook (it is filed by English topic: opensFor in src/resources/logos.ts)
src/Reader.tsx # a tap on a word of an 'hbo' chapter is setLookup like a Greek word's (no rewriting to the English sheet any more) and ReaderBody renders WordSheet with no onHelp and no onAskTerm for it
src/useLogosOn.ts # whether Logos is switched on, live (undefined while read): HebrewCard offers Turn on Logos (EnglishWordSheet.tsx TurnOnLogos) while it is off
src/data/lexicon.ts frequency.ts # loadLexicon(language) / lookupLemma(lemma, language) / loadFrequency(language) / rankOf / isProperNoun take 'grc' (the default) or 'hbo' and load lexicon.json / lexicon-hbo.json, frequency.json / frequency-hbo.json lazily, one request each; forgetLexicon / forgetFrequency drop both; tests/support/chapter-fetch.ts answers the Hebrew files too
src/nav/links.ts # findWord(text, lexicon, hebrew?) finds 'H7225' (h7225, H07225), the pointed lemma, or the lemma with its points off (bare() strips every mark, Hebrew points and accents too; the first lemma in lexicon-hbo.json's order wins when two bare to the same letters); isHebrewText(text) says whether a link names a Hebrew word (an H number or Hebrew letters); lemmaSheet({lemma, strongs: 'H…', gloss}) makes a one-word chapter of language 'hbo' (no transliteration, parse or definition); src/nav/LinkOpener.tsx loads the Hebrew lexicon only for such a link. There is no word field in the app: #/?word=ראשית (and web+lampas:word:H7225) is the typed path. tests/unit/links.test.ts
src/resources/logos.ts # Logos for a Hebrew word (docs/resources.md): wordLanguage(word) is 'hbo' for an H number (or Hebrew letters with no number); opensFor offers a lexicon of lang 'he' to a Hebrew word only, a Greek one to a Greek word only except the three Strong's dictionaries (key 'strongs': NASB Dictionaries, New Strong's, the Concise Dictionary), which open ref=HebrewStrongs.<n> for an H word; Bible Word Study is lbs/he/<consonants> (consonants() takes the points off); a lexicon filed by lemma gets hw=<consonants>. tests/unit/logos-links.test.ts, catalogue.test.ts
data/logos/catalogue-rules.json # eight lexicons with lang 'he' (BDB pinned, DBL Hebrew, TWOT, Gesenius, Abridged BDB, TLOT, Lexham Research Lexicon of the OT, Lexham Analytical Lexicon of the OT): `npm run logos:catalogue -- <csv>` writes src/resources/catalogue/; ResourceLang gains 'he', hidden at Just English and listed after the English ones like 'grc' (resourcesForLevel)
```
