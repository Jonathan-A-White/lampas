# Demo: Lampas listen-through-the-Bible plan (mw-umv8sp.6)

The epic mw-umv8sp gave Lampas a reading plan for listening to the Bible in Audible: a **Plan chip** in the Reader's row says
what is next and how far you are, **Start a listening plan** sets where you begin, a short **Off to Audible** sheet records
when you set off, and on your return **Where did you get to?** proposes the chapter and marks it done. This is the Governor's
check on his phone. When it all behaves as written, he says "Looks good".

Needs the installed Lampas on an Android phone with Chrome and the Audible app, on the updated version (reload when the app
says "Update ready, tap to reload").

## The steps

1. Clean start: open **Settings** (the gear), then **About**, and tap the version number seven times: a note says **Developer
   mode is on**. Back in **Settings**, under **Developer** tap **Reset the listening plan, for the demo**: **Reading plan**
   goes **Off**. (Do this again to run the demo from the top.)
2. Open the Reader. In the row under the title the **Plan chip** reads **Listen through the Bible**. Tap it: **Start a
   listening plan** opens with one plan, **The whole Bible** (**Settings** has the same entry under **Reading plan**). Tap
   **The whole Bible**; in **Where are you starting?** tap **Leviticus**, then **Chapter 1**. The chip now reads **Next:
   Leviticus 1** with **90 of 1,189** (Genesis and Exodus count as done).
3. Tap the chip. **Off to Audible: Leviticus 1** says "When you are back, tell me where you got to." with one big **Go**. Tap
   **Go**: Audible opens at Leviticus 1.
4. Listen for a while, then come back to Lampas. **Where did you get to?** says how long ago you set off and "I think you
   got to" a chapter. Under **Got further** are the chapters around the guess (the guess is marked **my guess**), and below
   them **Pick it myself**, **Stopped partway** and **Send a screenshot** (or **Needs a licence** with no licence).
5. Tap **Got further: Leviticus 3**. The sheet closes and the chip reads **Next: Leviticus 4** with **93 of 1,189**.
6. Tap the chapter title at the top of the Reader to open the picker, then **Leviticus**: chapters **1**, **2** and **3** are
   marked done and **4** is marked next. He says "Looks good".
