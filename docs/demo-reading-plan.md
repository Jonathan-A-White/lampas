# Demo: Lampas listen-through-the-Bible plan (mw-umv8sp.6)

The epic mw-umv8sp gave Lampas a reading plan for listening to the Bible in Audible: the **Reading plan** piece of **Today** (opened by the **Today** chip in the Reader's row) says
what is next and how far you are, **Start a listening plan** sets where you begin, a short **Off to Audible** sheet records
when you set off, and on your return **Where did you get to?** proposes the chapter and marks it done. This is the Governor's
check on his phone. When it all behaves as written, he says "Looks good".

Needs the installed Lampas on an Android phone with Chrome and the Audible app, on the updated version (reload when the app
says "Update ready, tap to reload").

## The steps

1. Clean start: open **Settings** (the gear), then **About**, and tap the version number seven times: a note says **Developer
   mode is on**. Back in **Settings**, under **Developer** tap **Reset the listening plan, for the demo**: **Reading plan**
   goes **Off**. (Do this again to run the demo from the top.)
2. Open the Reader and tap the **Today** chip in the row under the title. **Reading plan** says **No reading plan is started.**
   Tap **Start a plan**: **Start a listening plan** opens with one plan, **The whole Bible** (**Settings** has the same entry under **Reading plan**). Tap
   **The whole Bible**; in **Where are you starting?** tap **Leviticus**, then **Chapter 1**. **Reading plan** now reads **Next:
   Leviticus 1** with **90 of 1,189 chapters done** (Genesis and Exodus count as done).
3. Tap **Listen in Audible**. **Off to Audible: Leviticus 1** says "When you are back, tell me where you got to." with one big **Go**. Tap
   **Go**: Audible opens at Leviticus 1.
4. Listen for a while, then come back to Lampas. **Where did you get to?** says how long ago you set off and "I think you
   got to" a chapter. Under **I stopped in** are the chapters around the guess (the guess is marked **my guess**), and below
   them **Pick it myself**, **Send a screenshot** (or **Needs a licence** with no licence) and **Stopped partway, the plan stays
   at Leviticus 1**.
5. Tap **Stopped in: Leviticus 3**. The sheet closes and **Today** (tap the **Today** chip) reads **Next: Leviticus 3** with **92 of 1,189 chapters done**: a chapter
   you stopped in is not done, and your next listen starts at its beginning. (**Finished Leviticus 1** is the way to say you
   heard a chapter to its end.)
6. Tap the chapter title at the top of the Reader to open the picker, then **Leviticus**: chapters **1** and **2** are
   marked done and **3** is marked next. He says "Looks good".
