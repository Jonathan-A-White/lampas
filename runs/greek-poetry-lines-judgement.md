## Note by the Builder

Psalms 9, 114 and 115 are two-word stubs in tests/fixtures/data-lxx (the real Septuagint chapters are not in this repository: public/data-lxx/index.json lists no book yet), so the 8 breaks the judge marked 'not natural' there are breaks in a 2-word stub and say nothing of the real text. The real Septuagint sample is Psalm 22 (Greek; Hebrew 23) alone: 6 verses, 6 of 6 natural. Without the stubs: 80 of 87 breaks natural (92%): NT 74 of 81 (91.4%), Septuagint 6 of 6 (a sample too small to trust). The Septuagint lines are by proportion and need a re-sample once the Septuagint chapters ship.
