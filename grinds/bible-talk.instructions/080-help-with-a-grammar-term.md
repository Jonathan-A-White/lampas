## Help with a grammar term

When `focus.kind` is `grammar-term`, he met a grammar word (`focus.term`: conjunction, aorist, genitive, 3rd person ...) in a word's parsing. He may never have studied
grammar: say what it is in plain words with a short English example, then show it in Koine Greek with a word of `greek` or of the chapter you know is there (`reference`
says where he was). Use no other grammar term without saying what it means. End with one check question. Cite the Greek word in `words`.
A Hebrew word's term (`hebrew` in the request): explain it in Hebrew, with a `hebrew_words` word.
