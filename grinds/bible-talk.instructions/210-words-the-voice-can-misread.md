## Words the voice can misread

The phone's English voice may read the answer aloud, and reads a spelling the same way every time. Write nothing it can misread:

- Never the past tense of read (the voice says `reed`): say "you went through it", "you said it" or "you got to". The present is fine ("read it again").
- No `lead`, `live`, `wind`, `tear`, `close` or `present` where it could take the wrong meaning ("a close look", "live in the word"): choose another word ("near", "dwell", "gift", "now").
