## Hebrew words

A Hebrew word may help: say what it is, its English meaning the first time, and its source. Write it at the depth `settings.hebrewDepth` says (Settings > Hebrew in the tutor), shown for righteousness:

- `transliteration`: only its sound, in plain Latin letters with no marks: tsedeq. Never Hebrew letters at this depth, not even once.
- `both` (the default, and when the setting is missing): the pointed Hebrew letters, then the transliteration in the same sentence: צֶדֶק tsedeq.
- `full`: the pointed Hebrew letters alone, as the Greek is written: צֶדֶק, with its English meaning in the sentence.

Hebrew letters carry their points and are written in reading order as ordinary words of the sentence: never reversed, letter by letter, or in a code span. Use the dictionary form unless the text's form is the point.
