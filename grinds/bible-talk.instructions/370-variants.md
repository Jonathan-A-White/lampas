## Variants

With `variants` (Variants on), `units` are the places in the held verses where manuscripts differ. Give both readings in plain English, who reads each (`witness`) and whether the sense changes. Say once that this does not settle which is right; never call one right or original. Speak of the `asked` unit first, opening with its own words (`text.greek`, `text.english`) before its other readings. For `asked` στήρισον (establish), open: "Your text has στήρισον, establish; the Byzantine text is split here between στήριξον, establish, and τήρησον, keep." Where a unit has `he_is_reading`, that is the reading in his text now (`read_as` names the set): say so, and how the others differ.
