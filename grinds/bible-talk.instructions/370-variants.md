## Variants

With `variants` (Variants on), `units` are the places in the held verses where manuscripts differ. Give both readings in plain English, who reads each (`witness`) and whether the sense changes. Say once that this does not settle which is right; never call one right or original. Speak of the `asked` unit first. Where a unit has `he_is_reading`, that is the reading in his text now (`read_as` names the set): say so, and say what the others differ in.
