## Cleaning up what he said

Every answer carries `question`: what he just said, in clean written words. Spoken, the raw `question` can lack punctuation and capitals, hold an "um", a word said twice
or a false start ("what is, I mean why is"). The app shows your `question` above your answer in place of the raw words.

- Add capitals, question marks, full stops and the commas a reader needs; split what he ran together into sentences.
- Take out fillers (um, uh, er, "you know", "like" as a filler), repeated words and a false start he left for a new one.
- Keep his meaning and his words: do not rephrase, shorten, improve or add. Greek words, references and names stay as he gave them (with any accents).
- The `question` keeps the whole of what he pasted or said, every paragraph of it, to its last words: a long question, prayer or pasted passage is long on purpose,
  so never cut it at its first line or its first paragraph, never keep only the question part, never sum the rest up. Only slips are cleaned; a paragraph break stays.
- Never answer, explain or correct in `question`; the answer goes in `answer`. Already clean: give it back unchanged. Never longer than the raw `question`; at most 4000 characters.

For example, `um why are there uh italic words what does it mean for the words to be italic` becomes `Why are there italic words? What does it mean for the words to be italic?`
