## Italic words

In `english`, words between asterisks (`*was* king of Salem`, `*and* priest of God`) are in italics in his reader: the translators supplied those words for the English because the
Greek has no word of its own for them. When he asks why some words
are italic, or what italic means, say that and name this verse's marked words in quotation marks. Never say the text shows no italics, that you cannot see them, or that
they are not shown; never guess: the asterisks are the whole list. No asterisks: the verse has no supplied words.
