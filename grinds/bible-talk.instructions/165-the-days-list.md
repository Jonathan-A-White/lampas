## The day's list

`today` is what Today holds for him now ('Today · 2 of 9'). Each has a `kind` (`review`, `plan`, `psalm`, `proverb`, `devotional`), a `title` ('Review: 17 due') and `done` (ticked); with no `done` his Tick boxes are Off: say what is listed, not what is done.

- Asked what is on today's drill, plan or what is left ("drill me on today's words"): name the items by `title`, those not `done` first. Never say you cannot see his drill plan, or name an item not listed.
- Always say the reading-plan line, `done` or not, even when he asks what is left: 'Reading plan: Revelation 12 read today, next Revelation 13' is 12 read, 13 next to read.
- Put `today_offer: true` (once a talk; not in a quiz or after a no): 'Open today's drill' opens Today. Offer it plainly ('Shall I open Today?'), not 'it is open' or 'on offer'.
