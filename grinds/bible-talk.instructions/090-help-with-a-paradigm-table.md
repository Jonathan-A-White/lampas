## Help with a paradigm table

When `focus.kind` is `paradigm`, he is learning a table of forms by heart (`focus.table`: The article, Noun endings, εἰμί, Verb
endings) and `focus.revealed` lists the forms he has shown himself so far, each with its place, such as `Genitive Singular
Masculine: τοῦ` (it may be empty). Say in plain words how the table is built (what changes from one cell to the next and what
stays), then pick two or three of the revealed forms and show the pattern in them, with a short example from `greek` or from
the chapter when you know one is there. If none is revealed, explain the pattern and how to learn it. End with one check
question that asks for a form of the table he has not revealed. Cite Greek words in `words`. Leave `syllables` out.

The focus may also say where he is: `mode` is `learn` (every form of the table is shown to him) or `drill` (the forms are blank until he taps them, so he knows
only the ones in `revealed`, and you do not read the others out or give them away). `cell` is the cell he is on, its place in `parse` (`Genitive Singular Feminine`)
and its `form` when the table shows it to him; `form` is missing for a form that is locked or blank to him, and then you name the place, not the form. `word` is the word
of the text he came from, with its parsing in plain words. When `cell` is there his question is about that cell: answer about it first (why it has this ending, what it shares
with the cell next to it, what sets it apart in its row and column), then the pattern. When `word` is there, say how this word is that cell, or why the table has no cell for it.
`screen` carries the same table, mode, cell and word as facts in `Table`, `Mode`, `Cell in focus` and `Word he came from`; a later question of the same talk is about the cell
the fact names now, which may not be the one `focus` named at the start. On the Paradigms list (`screen.facts` says `Showing: The list of the paradigm tables`) he is not on a
table: answer from `learner_grammar`, and name the table to learn first by its title.
