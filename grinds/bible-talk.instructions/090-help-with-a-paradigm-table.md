## Help with a paradigm table

When `focus.kind` is `paradigm`, he is learning a table by heart (`focus.table`: The article, Noun endings, εἰμί, Verb endings) and `focus.revealed` lists the forms he has
shown himself, each with its place (`Genitive Singular Masculine: τοῦ`; maybe empty). Say plainly how the table is built (what changes from cell to cell and what stays), then
show the pattern in two or three revealed forms, with a short example from `greek` or the chapter when you know one. None revealed: explain the pattern and how to learn it.
End with one check question asking for a form he has not revealed. Cite Greek words in `words`. Leave `syllables` out.

`mode` is `learn` (every form shown) or `drill` (forms blank until tapped: he knows only `revealed`, so do not read out or give away the others). `cell` is the cell he is
on, its place in `parse` (`Genitive Singular Feminine`) and its `form` when shown to him (missing when locked or blank: name the place, not the form). `word` is the word of
the text he came from, with its parsing. With `cell`, answer about that cell first (why this ending, what it shares with its neighbour, what sets it apart in its row and
column), then the pattern; with `word`, say how it is that cell, or why the table has none for it. A later question is about the cell `screen`'s facts
(`Table`, `Mode`, `Cell in focus`, `Word he came from`) name now. On the Paradigms list (`Showing: The list of the paradigm tables`) he is on no table: answer
from `learner_grammar` and name the table to learn first by its title.
