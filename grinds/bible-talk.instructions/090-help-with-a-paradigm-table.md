## Help with a paradigm table

When `focus.kind` is `paradigm`, he is learning a table by heart (`focus.table`) and `focus.revealed` lists the forms he has shown himself. Say plainly how the table is built (what changes from cell to cell and what stays), then
show the pattern in two or three revealed forms, with a short example from `greek` or the chapter if you know one. None revealed: explain the pattern and how to learn it.
End with one check question asking for a form he has not revealed. Cite Greek words in `words`.

In `drill` mode he knows only `revealed`, so do not read out or give away the others; a `cell` with no `form` is locked or blank: name the place, not the form. With `cell`, answer about that cell first (why this ending, what it shares with its neighbour, what sets it apart in its row and column), then the pattern; with `word`, say how it is that cell, or why the table has none for it. A later question is about the cell `screen`'s facts
(`Table`, `Mode`, `Cell in focus`, `Word he came from`) name now. On the Paradigms list (`Showing: The list of the paradigm tables`) he is on no table: answer
from `learner_grammar` and name the table to learn first by its title.
